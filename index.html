Jiahui (Kent) Huang

I am a PhD student at the University of Waterloo under Matthew Satriano. Previous to this, I was a graduate student at ETH Zurich, under Arkadij Bojko.

Research Interest

I study algebraic geometry and motivic integration, and focus mainly on the applications of arc spaces to singularity theory. Recently, I am working on Batyrev's conjecture on non-negativity of stringy Hodge numbers.

Publications and preprints

Publications

  • Equivariant Segre and Verlinde invariants for Quot schemes Moduli, 2 (2025).

    Arkadij Bojko and Jiahui Huang.

    We provide universal power series structures for surface equivariant Segre and Verlinde invariants, including reduced invariants, with conjectural fourfold analogues.

  • What fraction of an Sn-orbit can lie on a hyperplane? Linear Algebra and Its Applications, 613 (2021).

    Jiahui Huang, David McKinnon, and Matthew Satriano.

    A bound for hyperplane sections of Sn-orbits is sharp when n or n-1 is prime.

Preprints

  • A complete classification of negative stringy Hodge numbers (2026).

    Jiahui Huang and Matthew Satriano.

    We classify when stringy Hodge numbers are always non-negative, with and without polynomiality of the stringy E-function.

  • A cohomological interpretation for stringy Hodge numbers (2026).

    Jiahui Huang, Matthew Satriano, and Jeremy Usatine.

    We define stringy cohomology for smooth Artin stacks which recovers the stringy Hodge–Deligne invariant of every log-terminal variety and agrees with orbifold cohomology for tame stacks.

  • Contact loci of semihomogeneous singularities (2025).

    Eduardo de Lorenzo Poza and Jiahui Huang.

    Arc-Floer conjecture is proved for semihomogeneous singularities with certain degree restrains. This is the first family of positive results for the conjecture in infinitely many dimensions. We then resolve the embedded Nash problem for all semihomogeneous singularities.
