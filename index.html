I am a PhD student at the University of Waterloo under Matthew Satriano. Previous to this, I was a graduate student at ETH Zurich, under Arkadij Bojko.
I study algebraic geometry and motivic integration, and focus mainly on the applications of arc spaces to singularity theory. Recently, I am working on the construction of a new cohomology theory for Artin stacks.
